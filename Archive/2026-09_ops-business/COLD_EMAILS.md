### Follow-up cadence (all templates)

No reply in 7 days → one follow-up on the same thread, one line: *"Bumping this up — happy to send a 3-minute Loom instead of a call if that's easier."* No reply after that → log as "cold, no response" and move on. Revisit in 60 days **only if a real number changed** — a new paying customer or a retention figure is the best second touch there is. A second email with the same facts is worse than no second email.

### Never in any of these emails

- **Any reference to Y Combinator.** Applied and rejected for Fall 2026; it is not a signal in either direction. If asked directly in a meeting: *"Applied for Fall 2026, didn't get in"* — one sentence, then move on.
- **A different cap for a different investor.** $3.5M, every time, or the round is unclosable the moment two of them compare notes.
- **"Nobody else is doing this."** Screener is doing it. The honest version — *they meter it because their cost scales per document; ours doesn't* — is a stronger claim and survives scrutiny.
