Anvest

Data provenance and methodology

Anvest Fintech Private Limited. Prepared for diligence. Version 1 Nov 2026. Evidence snapshot 20 Oct 2026, 12:51 IST, unless a date is given.

Anvest exists to give ordinary investors the tools an institutional desk has. For that to be safe, the data has to be right and the limits have to be stated. This memo sets out where the data comes from, how an answer is produced, what has been tested, and what is still open.

1. Data sources

  • Statutory filings and XBRL. Results, annual reports, shareholding patterns and announcements filed with NSE and BSE under SEBI's listing rules. These are public disclosures. Commercial reuse is still subject to each source's terms and to legal review.
  • End-of-day prices. NSE and BSE daily settlement files. Paid redistribution of price data needs exchange or vendor licensing, which the round's $65K compliance and data allocation budgets for before the paywall goes live.
  • Scanned filings. Many statutory results arrive as scans. In a pilot of 21 NSE filings from 9 banks (Q1 FY27), all ten tier-1 statutory results were scans. The embedded text layer can be wrong while looking right, so trust is decided by how the text entered the PDF, not by how it reads.
  • Funds, bonds and offer documents. AMFI scheme data and NAVs, government and corporate bond data including the RBI G-Sec benchmark, and IPO offer documents filed with SEBI and the exchanges.
  • Source access. BSE's API and MCX's feed deny automated access today. Affected data is labelled or hidden rather than estimated.
  • Broker account data. None today. Any pilot would need an approved access path, user consent, agreed retention and the partner's security review.

4. How an answer is produced

StepWhat happens
InterpretThe model classifies the question and resolves the company and period.
RetrieveEvidence comes from the warehouse and the filing index, through the same services the product pages use.
ComputeCode does the arithmetic. The model does not calculate.
ValidateNumeric checks compare each figure with retrieved values. A figure that cannot be reconciled is repaired or removed before display. This reduces risk and does not prove an answer correct.
CiteAnswers link to the filing. Citations are at filing level, not page level. Screening answers show the rule rather than a citation.

Reported line items are read from the filing. Derived ratios (ROE, ROCE, debt-to-equity) go through identity checks and cross-validation, and sector rules apply: ROCE is withheld for lenders. Corrections ship as versioned releases, and point-in-time history is preserved rather than overwritten.

6. Regulatory position

AreaPosition
Advice boundaryProduct rules, not only disclaimers: no buy, sell or hold calls, no target prices, no personalised investment advice. Planning tools do arithmetic on the user's own inputs and do not name securities.
RA and IA registrationNeither is held today. RA registration is funded in this round. Personal portfolio recommendations would need adviser registration and a separate decision. Counsel confirms the product's position.
AI in researchSEBI's December 2024 amendments require registered RAs and IAs to disclose AI use and hold them solely responsible for its output. Anvest shows sources and assumptions so a registered customer can review them; that does not itself establish compliance. SEBI RA Regulations.
Retail algosThe framework applies to all brokers from 1 April 2026. Black-box providers need RA registration and exchange empanelment. Anvest's backtests and paper trading are simulations; live strategies would need legal review, partner-broker controls and approvals. SEBI circular.
