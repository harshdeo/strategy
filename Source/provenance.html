1Purpose and scope

This memo describes where Anvest's data comes from, how it stays current, and how answers are grounded and verified. It is written for a technical or investment diligence reader.

2Data sources

  • End-of-day prices (bhavcopy): NSE and BSE publish daily settlement files. Public availability does not by itself establish commercial redistribution rights. Applicable exchange permissions and terms must be confirmed before paid redistribution or activation of a licensed feed.
  • Statutory filings and XBRL: quarterly and annual results, annual reports, shareholding patterns and corporate announcements filed with NSE and BSE under SEBI's listing obligations. The files are publicly accessible; reuse, extraction and commercial redistribution rights remain subject to source terms and legal review.
  • Scanned filings, made searchable: many small- and mid-cap filings are scanned PDFs rather than machine-readable XBRL. Anvest runs them through OCR so they are searchable and citable on the same footing as structured filings.
  • Funds, bonds and offer documents: mutual fund NAVs and scheme data (AMFI), government and corporate bond data including the RBI G-Sec benchmark, and IPO offer documents filed with SEBI and the exchanges.
  • Broker account data (future partner scope): any pilot would require an explicitly approved access path, user consent, agreed data minimisation and retention, and partner security review. No live Zerodha integration or partner authorization is represented by this memo.

4Update cadence and infrastructure

A primary evening ingestion run, a morning catch-up run and intraday supplemental refreshes support updates through the latest completed trading session. Production runs on Google Cloud in the Mumbai region with the web application on Vercel. Scheduled database backups use encrypted off-site storage and generation integrity checks. A full restore of the migrated production warehouse has not yet been proven.

Live-data roadmap: first, live filing alerts from the exchange announcement feeds; second, live quotes and portfolio tracking through each user's own broker connection (for example Kite Connect), with an exchange-licensed delayed feed for public pages. The pipeline accepts these sources alongside the nightly batch.

5How an answer is produced

InterpretThe model classifies intent and resolves companies and periods.
RetrieveEvidence comes from the warehouse and the filing index.
ComputeDeterministic engines do the arithmetic: the model never calculates.
ValidatePost-generation numerical checks compare claims with retrieved values and the claim ledger.
CiteEach figure links to its filing; gaps are stated.

The "grounded or silent" rule is enforced in retrieval and verification code. If a generated figure cannot be reconciled to retrieved evidence and the claim ledger, the response is repaired or suppressed before display. This control is not a claim that every possible numeric error has been eliminated.

6Verification of figures

Source and pipeline audit (20 October 2026): revenue and PAT extraction matched the original values in 50 of 50 distinct consolidated Q2 FY27 NSE XBRL filings. This is an extraction audit, not a 50-answer quality benchmark. A separate four-question live spot-check and the proposed 50-prompt evaluation suite are distinct evidence; the proposed suite has not been run. In the TCS Q2 FY27 example, the answer labels PAT / total income (18.70% on ₹74,525 Cr) separately from PAT / revenue from operations (19.04% on ₹73,188 Cr), and reports the 67 bps YoY operating-margin decrease (25.47% vs. 26.14%) against the NSE filing dated 8 October 2026.

Reported line items (revenue, profit, assets, equity and similar) are read directly from the source filing and can be checked against the filing PDF or XBRL tag in one click.

Derived ratios (ROE, ROCE, ROA, debt-to-equity and similar) go through an active data-quality programme: automated identity checks (for example, that quarterly figures reconcile to the reported annual figure), cross-validation against independently recomputed values, and sampled comparison with an external public reference. Sector rules are applied deliberately: ROCE, for instance, is withheld for lenders rather than computed. Formula changes follow internal validation and release gates; coverage and freshness remain specific to each dataset.

Corrections ship as versioned releases with an internal change log, and point-in-time history is preserved rather than overwritten. A public corrections page is on the roadmap. The current internal data-quality report is available under a standard diligence arrangement.

8Data, evaluation and operational risk management

Risk FactorHow Anvest Handles It
Restated or revised exchange filingsPoint-in-time versioning preserves each filing as received; restatements are ingested as new versions without overwriting historical snapshots. Filing-date lineage is enforced in the warehouse.
Unreadable scanned tablesOCR pipelines reconstruct table structure from scanned PDFs. Where OCR confidence is below threshold or a filing is missing entirely (~4,500 company-years), the coverage gap is explicitly flagged to the user rather than interpolated or estimated.
Sector-specific accounting rulesSector guardrails suppress analytically invalid ratios (e.g. ROCE for banks and NBFCs where capital-adequacy accounting makes it meaningless). Natural-language screener requests are translated into deterministic SQL with sector filters applied.
Answer-quality evidenceThe 50/50 result covers revenue/PAT extraction only. The four-question live spot-check and the proposed 50-prompt golden suite have separate scopes; the suite remains unrun and has no pass rate. Missing, skipped, errored or unjudged cases must count as failures when it is executed.
Commercial data rightsPublic access does not establish commercial redistribution permission. Applicable exchange and source terms must be confirmed before paid redistribution; the model's compliance allocation is a planning assumption, not a license or approval.
Broker integration and privacyNo partner access or pilot is agreed. Before any pilot, Anvest and the partner must approve the access method, user consent, purpose, retention, security controls and incident responsibilities.
Restore readinessEncrypted off-site backup generations and integrity checks are scheduled. A full migrated-warehouse restore has not yet been proven; backup presence is not presented as restore certification.
