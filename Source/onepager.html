The problem

Institutions have analysts, terminals and quant desks. A retail investor or a two-person advisory firm has exchange PDFs (61,875 announcements in August 2026 alone), ratio screeners, spreadsheets and tips. Neither can afford the desk.

What we learned building it

Indian filings are not machine-ready, and a model pointed at them inherits every error.

  • XBRL dates a full-year column with the quarter's dates (RELIANCE Q4).
  • 15,899 cash-flow rows recovered from year-to-date blocks parsers reject, none failing reconciliation.
  • 3,608 source conflicts across 1,022 companies. ADANIPORTS FY25 profit: ₹1,09,197 Cr in a secondary source, ₹11,092 Cr as filed.
  • 10 of 10 tier-1 bank results in a sample were scans. City Union Bank's capital adequacy reads 21.73 on the page and 27.73 in the hidden text.

The product, live at anvest.in

For a question: ask, get a screen with rules you can read, backtest it with brokerage, STT, stamp duty and slippage, then paper-trade it. On 19 Oct one prompt produced a 516-stock screen, a one-year costed backtest and a 10-stock paper basket.

For a beginner: a portfolio simulator in everyday words, retirement and goal planners that show their assumptions, fund comparisons and IPO pages. One conversation across all of these is being built and ships once it clears evaluation.

The model reads the question; code does the arithmetic. No buy, sell or hold calls, and no target prices.
