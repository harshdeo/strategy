The problem & our earned insight

Research teams move between exchange filings, summary screeners, spreadsheets and backtest tools. Machine-readable filings can still have period and tagging problems; many disclosures also arrive as scanned PDFs.

Anvest's ingestion pipeline has recovered 15,899 cash-flow rows from malformed XBRL and flagged 3,608 conflicting figures across 1,022 companies. A production audit counted 6,155,194 indexed passages across 633,688 distinct document IDs on 20 Oct 2026.

The product (live at anvest.in)

Filing answers link to their evidence; unsupported coverage is stated. Deterministic code performs calculations.

Why now

  • SEBI's December 2024 research-analyst amendments address disclosure of the extent of AI use in research.
  • The retail algo framework applies to brokers from 1 Apr 2026, with specific requirements for live algos and black-box providers. Anvest backtests and paper trading are simulations today.
  • These rules shape our product and partner roadmap; they do not establish demand or regulatory approval for Anvest.

Product position

Anvest combines filing citations, screening, costed backtests and paper workflows in one product. A review of public product pages on 19 Oct 2026 was directional, not a hands-on benchmark; a feature not shown publicly is not assumed unavailable.
