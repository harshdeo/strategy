**Internal · reviewed 20 Oct 2026. No emails sent or applications submitted.**

Start now with suitable pre-seed investors. Work through small batches so feedback improves the next message. Do not broadcast to all 50 tracker entries: several are platforms, later-stage funds, duplicate personal routes or unnamed prospecting pools. Fit and timing below are recommendations, not probabilities of funding.

## First actions

1. **Before attaching a PDF:** reconcile the corpus counts against the newer audit described below. You can start introduction requests immediately. **20–22 October: five full pitches once the attachment is corrected.** Eximius and All In first, then Titan, Inuka and Gemba. Attach the current seed PDF; include one specific reason for that fund. A paid pilot is not a blanket prerequisite for these early-stage conversations.
2. **23–29 October: nine more funds.** India Quotient, Zeropearl, Whiteboard, z21, First Cheque, Rainmatter, SenseAI, Better and 3one4. Use the official form where that is the published route. Ask actual mutual contacts for introductions in parallel, with a 48-hour limit on waiting.
3. **30 October–6 November: twelve selected additional funds.** Use the wave-3 rows below. Prioritize a strong genuine introduction sooner if available. Review the first calls for buyer, market-size and evidence questions; do not change facts to suit a fund.
4. **Keep customer sales running daily.** Approach named professional firms for customer discovery and paid pilots. No pilot is represented as started by this plan. Record customer evidence separately from investor conversations.
5. **Follow up on day 7 and, if useful, day 14.** Use the same email thread. Stop after two unanswered follow-ups, a pass or an opt-out. After a reply, agree and record the next step. Later updates need real progress.

Reserve roughly 45–60 minutes per working day for researched first contacts and follow-ups, plus a separate customer-sales block. These are workload choices, not response-rate or fundraising forecasts. A few suitable investors with substantive conversations are more useful than a long sent-email count.

## What to send and say

- **From:** harsh@anvest.in. Use one recipient/route per fund; do not CC several partners. A warm introduction is useful when the introducer knows your work. No introducer has been verified or recorded by this refresh.
- **First email:** 120–180 words, a specific fund opener, one recurring buyer problem, a live product link, the $500K ask and a request for a 20-minute demo. Current seed PDF only; one-pager optional. Do not attach the tracker, strategy plans or full model. Use the ready drafts in `Anvest_Outreach_Emails.md`.
- **Round wording:** raising $500K, first close $400K, room to $700K; proposed $3.5M post-money cap. Financing structure and instrument need counsel and agreed terms. A fund’s published cheque range is not an offer. Do not say the round is committed or that an allocation is filling without evidence.
- **Founder:** employed today; full-time on Anvest from the close. Explain notice period and IP position accurately. Do not claim a signed assignment, clean cap table or employer clearance unless the records support it.
- **Product:** cited filing research for Indian professionals, with screening, costed backtests and paper trading. Sell a repeatable professional research job first; the deck explains the longer-term breadth.
- **Meeting:** show one cited answer and its source, then an editable public screen. Check actual production routes before each call. Use a real recorded walkthrough as backup. The latest backlog records production closure of four broker bugs. Cold first-run latency and the virtual-cash tile remain; do not promise every route is certified.
- **After a meeting:** within 24 hours, send the requested evidence and agree a dated next step. Share the operating model/provenance memo on request; distinguish assumptions from actual contracts.

## How to use the Excel tracker

Filter `Investor Targets` by Wave 1, 2 or 3. Columns P–V hold person, email, route, primary source, review date and suggested opener. Keep one row per investment process. The ID in AC is stable; sort the whole table, not one column.

Before sending, confirm the published route still exists, personalize, and check the current PDF and real demo. After sending, enter **First sent (W)**. For a later touch enter **Last touch (X)** and update **Follow-ups sent (Y)**. **Next follow-up (AB)** is seven days after the effective touch, until two follow-ups or a recorded reply/pass/pause/commitment. A recorded reply/outcome in Z stops the automatic reminder; enter the agreed next action/date in M–N. No reminder sends anything.

Blue workflow fields are editable. Planned dates in N are proposed actions, never evidence that a pitch was sent. Statuses and actual send/touch dates remain unsent at delivery. Use Notes and Intro owner to record real relationships; do not count a fund, its founder and its platform as three leads.

The six existing tabs remain: actions, evidence/readiness, targets, round terms, timeline and sources. The round tab fixes the former USD/INR unit errors and draws financial outputs from the operating model. The proposed raise/cap ratio is illustrative; it does not calculate founder ownership.
